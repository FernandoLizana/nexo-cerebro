# Informed Consent Template (P9)

**Study:** NEXO Cognitive QA Human Interaction Study  
**Purpose:** Compare human software interaction with NEXO simulated agents on equivalent Web Lab tasks.

## What we collect
- Pseudonymous participant ID
- Task interactions (semantic actions, not passwords)
- Completion, errors, timing
- Optional subjective difficulty/frustration/confidence (1–5)

## What we do NOT collect
- Real passwords, payment data, government IDs, or clinical diagnoses

## Risks
Minimal — standard computer-based task interaction.

## Withdrawal
You may withdraw at any time without penalty. Request deletion of your data.

## Not a clinical diagnosis
This study does not assess medical or psychological conditions.
