# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the NEXO project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the scientific community
- Showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or insulting comments
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Shipping or advocating malware-like features (worms, silent install, unauthorized scanning, remote shell)
- Using the project to harm people or systems outside voluntary lab consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

For incidents, contact the maintainers privately. Maintainers will review and respond in a way they deem appropriate to the circumstances.

## Scope

This Code applies within project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.
